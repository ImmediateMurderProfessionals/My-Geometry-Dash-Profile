Bopping Spree

Bopping Spree is a platformer level inspired by the Super Mario Party minigame of the same name that I made in June 2026 before releasing on June 18, 2026. It was submitted to Arcadiac CC1, where it won 1st place, carrying a prize of $150.

Rating status

The level is unrated, and I am aiming for about a 4 moon rate. So far it has been sent to 10 moderators, of which, to my knowledge, 7 have sent the level and 2 have rejected it.
